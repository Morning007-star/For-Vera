Vivi,

I'm not always good at saying things out loud, so I built this instead. That is the part of me that comes easiest.

We have known each other since primary school, almost seven years now. It still amazes me that after everything, the person I fell for was you. We are coming up on two years together, and even though we have not managed to meet in person since we started dating, you have never once felt far from me.

You are my first love, and I do not say that lightly. I know we have had our differences lately, and I take that seriously. I want to be a better man for you, every day, not just on the good ones.

This page will not fix everything, but I wanted you to have something that is just yours. Something you can open whenever you need reminding that I am serious about you, about us, and about the future I want to build with you.

I intend on marrying you one day, Vera. Until then, this is yours to open whenever you miss me.

Rey
